Architecture & Features

What Kairogram Contains

Engineered from the ground up for high-fidelity offline Bible study, live sermons, and spiritual discipline.

Real-Time Voice-to-Verse AI

Listens continuously to live preaching or scripture readings. Employs a sentence buffer and phonetic parser to recognize citations in real-time, popping up the exact verse instantly.

Dual-Page Paperback Spread

On Android tablets and landscape displays, the reader unfolds into a physical open-book spread with authentic spine stitching, drop-cap opening verses, EB Garamond serif type, and 3D page curl animations.

Interlinear Strong's Concordance

Dive into original biblical languages. Tap any verse to unpack Hebrew (Westminster Leningrad Codex with Nikkud) or Greek (Scrivener 1894 Textus Receptus) alongside Strong's numbers and transliterations.

Synchronized Devotionals Hub

Access daily Christian devotionals including Open Heavens, Daily Manna, Rhapsody of Realities, Sunday School manuals, and MFM Devotionals—cached offline with cloud persistence.

Contextual Notes & Highlights

Pen personal sermon notes directly linked to scriptures. Highlights, bookmarks, and attached notes appear with indicator badges right on the verse row.

High-Fidelity Text-to-Speech

Listen to verses or chapters read aloud with natural pacing. Audio controls let you play, pause, and follow along while on the move or in personal meditation.

Quick Setup

How to Install Kairogram on Android

Follow these 4 simple steps to install the APK directly onto your tablet or smartphone in under a minute.

01

Download the APK

Tap the Download APK button on this site. Your device will save KairoGram.apk (approx. 34.4 MB) to your Downloads folder.

02

Allow Unknown Sources

If prompted by Chrome or your browser that “File might be harmful”, tap Download anyway. When opening, if asked to allow installation, tap Settings → Allow from this source.

Tip: This is a standard Android security message for apps installed outside the Google Play Store.
03

Tap Install & Launch

Tap Install on the package installer prompt. Once complete, tap Open to launch Kairogram.

04

Grant Microphone Access

When prompted, allow Microphone Permission. This enables the live speech recognition engine to listen to sermon audio and detect scripture.
